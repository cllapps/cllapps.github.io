3. AI-Powered Features and Data Handling

To enhance user experience, certain features within our app may be powered by Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies provided by third-party service providers, such as Google Gemini or similar APIs. These AI-powered features primarily include scanning and interpreting QR codes and other related data.

How AI Processes Your Information

When you use the AI-powered scanning feature, scanned content (such as QR codes or image keyframes) may be securely transmitted to our third-party AI providers for real-time analysis.

This data is used exclusively to deliver the requested results. While we and our providers do not perform long-term storage or use your data to train AI models, a temporary technical cache is maintained solely to ensure session stability and successful processing. Such cached data is encrypted and automatically deleted immediately after processing, or within a maximum of 48 hours.

Our providers are contractually prohibited from retaining this data beyond these technical requirements or sharing it with unauthorized parties.

Disclaimer on AI-Generated Content

AI-generated outputs are produced by probabilistic models and may contain inaccuracies. Users should verify critical information decoded from scans before taking action. We disclaim any liability for decisions or consequences resulting from the use of AI-generated content.

4. Data Security and Storage

We follow "Privacy by Design" principles and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards.

Minimization: We do not require account registration and do not collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII) for general App use.

Local Storage: Most data (scanned history, app settings) is stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App cache or uninstalling the App.

AI Data Retention: Data uploaded for AI processing is encrypted during transmission and is temporarily cached by third-party service providers for technical purposes, in accordance with their privacy agreements.
